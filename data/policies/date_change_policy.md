# Effective Date Change Rules

A date-change request may be auto-processed when the customer clearly states
a single new effective date and a valid policy number, with no conflicting
values in the transcript.

If the requested new effective date is within 24 hours of the call time,
the request must be escalated for supervisor approval, even if the evidence
is otherwise unambiguous, because same-day changes affect billing cutoffs.

If the customer provides a relative date only (e.g. "next Monday") without
ever stating an absolute date, and no absolute date can be reliably derived
from context in the transcript, the request should be escalated rather than
having the system guess a calendar date.
